Before using the result

  • Check factual errors, missing context, and inappropriate grading assumptions.
  • Use consistent criteria and explain departures. Human authority can still be exercised incorrectly.
  • Provide a review or appeal route. Consider accommodations and assessment conditions.
  • Do not convert score differences, language style, gaze, or experimental markers into automatic misconduct accusations.
  • Keep student answers, references, and examiner records private.
