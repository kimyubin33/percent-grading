# Related work

## Evidence-Centered Design

Mislevy, R. J., Almond, R. G., and Lukas, J. F. (2004). A Brief Introduction to Evidence-Centered Design. CSE Report 632.

https://cresst.org/publication/a-brief-introduction-to-evidence-centered-design/

ECD organizes assessment around evidentiary arguments. It motivates explicit links between responses and claims. This project does not implement the full ECD framework.

## LLM-as-a-Judge

Zheng, L., et al. (2023). Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685

The study discusses model-based evaluation and biases including position and verbosity. Its conversational evaluation findings do not validate this educational grading system.
